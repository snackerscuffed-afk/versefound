Common questions

Where does the Bible text come from?
From three public domain translations stored in the app: the World English Bible, the King James Version and the American Standard Version. AI never writes Bible text; it only helps choose passages and writes short notes marked as AI.

Does it work offline?
Reading, search, the daily verse, read aloud, bookmarks, highlights and your journal work offline. Finding passages for what you are facing and asking about a passage need an internet connection.

Is what I type private?
What you type is used to find passages and is not stored on our server. Your journal stays on your phone, encrypted. See the privacy policy: Privacy policy.

How many free answers do I get?
3 answers and 5 passage questions every day, shown on the Home screen. Verse Found Plus gives unlimited answers and questions.

How do I cancel Plus?
iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Verse Found. Android: Google Play, profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Verse Found. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I keep my journal when I change phones?
Export it from the My verses tab before you switch. The journal is stored only on your phone, so we cannot restore it for you.

The read aloud voice sounds robotic.
The app uses your phone's own voices. On iPhone, download a natural voice in Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, English (choose one marked Enhanced or Premium). The app uses it automatically.

I saw the crisis screen but I am fine.
The app errs on the side of safety. Tap "I'm safe, continue" to see your passages.
