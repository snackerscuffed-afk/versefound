Verse Found privacy policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Verse Found is made by the Verse Found team ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we keep as little as we can, your journal stays on your phone, and we never sell your data or show ads.

What stays on your phone

  • Your journal, bookmarks, highlights and notes. They are stored only on your phone, encrypted, and are never sent to us. If you delete the app or move to a new phone without exporting them, they are gone; we cannot recover them.
  • Your settings, such as translation, text size and appearance.
  • A random install ID. The app makes a random code the first time it runs. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. It is used to count free daily answers.

What is sent from your phone, and why

When you use "What are you facing?" or ask a question about a passage, the app sends to our server:

  • the words you typed (and, for a passage question, the passage reference),
  • the Bible translation you are reading,
  • your install ID and today's date (to count free answers).

Our server passes your words to our AI provider, Anthropic, to check them for safety and to choose passages. The answer comes back to your phone.

  • We do not store what you type. Our server uses it for that one request and does not write it to disk or to our logs.
  • Our logs keep only: a scrambled form of your install ID (it cannot be turned back into the ID), the time, how long the request took, its cost, and labels such as "answer" or "crisis screen shown". They do not contain your words.
  • Anthropic processes your words to give the answer, under its commercial terms. By default Anthropic does not use them to train its models, and it deletes them from its systems within 30 days. It may keep them longer, up to 2 years, only if its automated safety systems flag a request as breaking its usage policy, or where the law requires. See Anthropic's privacy center.
  • Our hosting provider, Fly.io, runs our server and processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver each request. See Fly.io's privacy statement.

Reading the Bible, search, the daily verse, read aloud, bookmarks, highlights and the journal all work on your phone and send nothing to us.

Purchases

If you buy Verse Found Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment card. We use RevenueCat to keep track of subscriptions on our behalf. It receives the purchase receipt, the product, the subscription status and your app's random install ID, and tells our server whether your install has Plus. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing your information for marketing.
  • No analytics tools that follow you across apps or websites.
  • No account, email address or sign in required.

Safety

If what you write suggests you may be in danger, the app shows ways to reach help (such as the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline). We do not contact anyone on your behalf, and we do not store what you wrote.

Children

Verse Found is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app has no accounts and does not ask for names, email addresses or other contact details. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • You can export your journal from the My verses tab at any time.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your phone.
  • Because we do not keep what you type or link your install ID to you, we have no personal file about you to show or delete. If you have questions, contact us below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
